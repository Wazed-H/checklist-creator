✓ Checklist Creator

How to use:
  1. Paste your comma-separated list below
  2. Click "Turn into Checklist"
  3. Check off items as you complete them
  4. Save your checklist to a named session when you want to keep it
⚠️ Warning: Do not include commas in your list items. Each item will be split by comma.
This site uses localStorage to save your sessions until you manually delete them.
